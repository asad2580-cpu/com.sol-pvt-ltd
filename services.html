Role 01

Data Entry

💻

What the Role Involves

Data Entry professionals are responsible for accurately inputting, updating, and maintaining information in company databases and systems. This is a back-office function critical to smooth business operations.

  • ✔  Entering data into spreadsheets and internal systems
  • ✔  Verifying accuracy of records and fixing errors
  • ✔  Managing and organising digital files
  • ✔  Generating basic reports from data sets
✅

Who Should Apply

This role is ideal for freshers and candidates with basic computer knowledge looking to start their career in a corporate environment.

  • ✔  Basic typing speed and computer literacy
  • ✔  Familiarity with MS Excel or Google Sheets
  • ✔  Attention to detail and accuracy
  • ✔  10+2 or graduate in any stream
Role 02

Receptionist & Front Desk

📞

What the Role Involves

Receptionists are the first point of contact for any organisation. The role requires strong communication, a professional demeanour, and the ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously.

  • ✔  Managing front desk and greeting visitors
  • ✔  Handling incoming calls and routing them
  • ✔  Scheduling appointments and managing calendars
  • ✔  Handling couriers, mail, and office coordination
✅

Who Should Apply

Best suited for candidates with good spoken English or Hindi, a pleasant personality, and an interest in client-facing roles.

  • ✔  Good verbal communication skills
  • ✔  Professional appearance and attitude
  • ✔  Basic computer and email knowledge
  • ✔  Graduate preferred, 10+2 also considered
Role 03

Document Verification

📄

What the Role Involves

Document Verification executives work in compliance, banking, fintech, and insurance sectors. They are responsible for reviewing and validating identity and financial documents submitted by customers or employees.

  • ✔  KYC document checking and validation
  • ✔  Cross-verifying submitted documents vs records
  • ✔  Flagging discrepancies and escalating issues
  • ✔  Maintaining verification logs and reports
✅

Who Should Apply

Ideal for detail-oriented candidates comfortable working in regulated environments like banking, insurance, or government-affiliated organisations.

  • ✔  Eye for detail and accuracy
  • ✔  Knowledge of common ID documents (Aadhaar, PAN etc.)
  • ✔  Basic computer skills
  • ✔  Graduate in any stream preferred
